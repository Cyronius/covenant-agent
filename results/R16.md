# R16: SPt on the options-off rows does not beat A0; tool backoff holds on every model

**Date:** 2026-09-26. **Branch:** `description-reading`.
**Spend:** $0.51: one secure-cloud A40 at $0.49/hr, rented at 21:32 UTC and
deleted at about 22:36. The balance went from $13.33 to $12.82.
**Everything the pod wrote is on the laptop** in
`models/tiny/runs/pod_s6off2/`: both runs' `best.pt`, `last.pt`, config and
curve, all eight generations, the logs and the pod console. All 23 files
were checked against the pod's listing by size before the pod was deleted.
**Picks up:** R15 "Next" 2, which is R14 next-run 1 plus a second A0 seed.

## The result

1. **SPt trained on the options-off rows is worse than A0 on the plain
   exam.** It scores 72.8%, against A0's 79.5% (seed 0) and 77.2% (seed 1).
   On the decoyed exam it is 8.8 points better (46.2% against 37.4%). This is
   the same trade R14 saw on S5's rows (71.7% against 73.9% plain), now
   larger. The reading stages help SPt tell a decoy from the real tool, and
   cost it accuracy on tasks without decoys.
2. **A0's seed spread on these rows is 2.3 points** (79.5% and 77.2%).
   Between two A0 seeds, SPt's shortfall is 4.4–6.7 points. That is outside
   the seed spread, but SPt has only one seed.
3. **Tool backoff helps every model, and loses nothing anywhere.**

   | model | exam | greedy | tool backoff | tasks gained / lost |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | A0 seed 0 (R14 run 3) | plain | 79.5% | **84.5%** | +101 / −0 (R15) |
   | A0 seed 1 | plain | 77.2% | 82.0% | +94 / −0 |
   | SPt | plain | 72.8% | 76.8% | +81 / −0 |
   | SPt | decoyed | 46.2% | 47.6% | +30 / −0 |

   The best planner for unseen worlds is unchanged: A0 seed 0 with backoff,
   at 84.5%. Across the two A0 seeds, backoff averages 83.3%.
4. **The seven failing worlds partly follow the seed.**
   - Five of R15's seven worlds still fail at seed 1: toy_store,
     ci_cd, radio_station, wedding_planning and animal_shelter.
   - Two recover: border_transit (46% to 10%) and comic_publisher (39% to
     10%).
   - Three worlds that seed 0 handled now fail on 14–16% of their tasks:
     winery, courthouse and food_bank.

   The overall rate of same-shape swaps hardly moves (7.0% and 7.5% of
   tasks). So a swap's rate is a property of the model and its training;
   where it lands is partly the world and partly the seed.
5. **SPt fails different worlds.** SPt fixes most of A0's seven: goal success
   there is 67.4%, against 55.5% and 59.5% for the two A0 seeds. But it
   fails others that A0 handles: donor_relations 34.7%, ad_platform 30.0%,
   parking_authority 26.5%, streaming_service 23.1%. Overall its swap rate
   is 9.6% of tasks, against A0's 7.0–7.5%.

goal success, with the same task ids within each column: plain 2,000,
decoyed 2,000, flip 472, test 1,000.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **A0** | S6 options-off | 1 | 77.2% | 82.0% | 37.2% | 23.7% | 94.5% |

SPt on options-off rows reaches none of R13's flip-probe score: 24.6%,
against 38.8%. The flip probe asks for a sibling the theme did not author
as the tool. That skill appears to come from training on decoys and twin
roles, not from the stages alone.

Validation loss does not predict the exam. It is measured on the training

yet scores 2.3 points lower on unseen worlds.

## What this changes

- **For the plain exam, stay with A0 and add tool backoff.** SPt belongs only
  where decoys are expected. There it is +8.8 points greedy (46.2% against
  37.4%) and +8.6 with backoff (47.6% against 39.0%).
- **R14 next-run 2 (the options put back one at a time) matters more now.**
  A0 barely gains from decoy training (R14: +1.2 on decoyed), and SPt
  without decoy training loses 14 points on the flip probe against R13's
  SPt. The open question is how much decoy training SPt needs to keep its
  decoy and flip skill without R13's 32-point plain-exam cost. A decoys-on-
  a-fraction arm is the natural first try.
- **Seeds:** differences under about 2.5 points between single A0 runs on
  these rows are within seed noise.
