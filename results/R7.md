# R7 — structural binding: the 9.4M tiny planner binds symbols, and writes working programs

**Status:** step 1 of `.claude/plans/npu-native-planner.md` passes its gate, by a
margin that makes the gate uninteresting. Tool-slot accuracy went from 6% against
5.6% chance (R3) to 99.5% on the test split and 98.0% on a world the model never
trained on. Compile rate went from 8.6% to 99.9%. One seed, so the spread is
unmeasured; the effect is far too large to be seed noise, and everything
downstream of it now depends on numbers that are closer to a ceiling than to a
floor.

**Date:** 2026-09-18. **Plan:** `.claude/plans/npu-native-planner.md`, sequence
step 1; `.claude/plans/canvas-field-access-split.md` for the tokenizer half.
**Code:** `models/tiny/`, `--binding structural`. **Model:** 9.4M parameters,
width 256: a 2-layer per-line encoder, a 1-layer sparse schema-graph pass, a
2-layer turn encoder, a 4-layer decoder over a 64-slot canvas. Two arms differ in
one line, whether decoder self-attention is causal. **Data:** `data/s5_plain.jsonl`,
same 90/5/5 split as R3 and the same held-out world (`bookstore`). **Trained:**
12 epochs, batch 64, `pad_weight` 0.5, one seed, on a rented GPU
(`models/tiny/run_step1.sh`). **Scored:** every program compiled by
`core.pipeline.build` and executed by `harness.run.run_task` against world state.
No number here comes from any other path.

## 1. What changed from R3

Three things, all representational, all in the plan's decisions 4, 6, 7 and 11.
Nothing about capacity: the model grew by 1.5M parameters, all of it in two extra
encoder layers.

- **One vector per schema line, not one token stream.** Each tool, field and
  constant line is encoded on its own; a sparse graph pass lets a tool see the
  fields its signature names and a field see its entity and its tools. The
  per-turn path reads about 50 line vectors plus the request instead of 1,100
  tokens of serialised schema.
- **Pointers, not a symbol vocabulary.** A canvas slot holds a keyword (62 fixed
  rows) or points at one of this task's line vectors, or at a register. There is
  no `T23` row to learn. An undeclared symbol has no vector and cannot be
  produced, which is the symbol half of the grammar for free.
- **`r0.F6` is two slots.** R3's output vocabulary held 115 register-dot-field
  compounds covering 708 of 2,064 field references, so a third of the field
  references were not symbol slots at all. Both bindings now split them
  (`corpus.program_tokens`), and the flat vocabulary fell from 500 entries to 401.

in the direction that keeps the pretrained text encoder off the table.

## 3. The control beats the diffusion arm by 10 points, and where

R3 could not compare the arms, because both were at the floor. At 99% binding the
comparison is finally about generation order, and the left-to-right arm wins:
98.6% against 88.9% goal on test, 96.4% against 82.5% on the held-out world.
More denoising steps close part of it (85.2% at one step to 88.9% at 32) and the
compiler in the sampling loop buys 2.1 points at 8 steps, but nothing reaches the
control.

The gap is concentrated, not spread. Levels 0 to 6, which are R3's pass bar:

| level | what it needs | control | diffusion, 32 steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | one call | 100% | 99% |
| 1 | a lookup then a call | 100% | 100% |
| 2 | filter then foreach | 98% | 78% |
| 3 | a compound filter predicate | **78%** | **16%** |
| 4 | field access into a call | 99% | 90% |
| 5 | an IF/ELSE branch | 96% | 67% |
| 6 | a format then a call | 100% | 79% |
| all 0 to 6 | | 96.2% | 76.7% |

Level 3 is a multi-term predicate: `FILTER r0 F12 LT D0 AND NOT F9 EQ B0 -> r1`.
Six slots that are only right together, and the diffusion arm fills them from
independent marginals at nearby steps. That is the same mechanism R3 guessed at
for the `EFFECTS` header, now visible on a level where the model is otherwise
competent, and it is the most specific thing known about what parallel filling
costs on this task. Of the 19.5 points the diffusion arm gives up on levels 0 to
6, level 3 is 41 of the 101 lost tasks, level 5 is 25, level 6 is 17 and level 2
is 11; levels 0, 1 and 4 cost it 8 between them.

**Neither arm passes R3's bar.** The bar is 100% on levels 0 to 6 under full
constrained decoding at 40M parameters or less; the control is at 96.2% under the
grammar's symbol half only, with level 3 at 78%. It is close enough that the bar
is now a target rather than a question.

## 4. What this settles for the NPU design, and what it does not

- **Bet 1, that a small resident block can do the task at all, is no longer
  open at 9.4M.** The plan's measured size curve had 7.9M at chance and nothing
  working under 350M. A 9.4M model now writes working programs for unseen
  worlds. The session-encode split is the reason, as the plan argued: the
  per-turn path never reads schema text.
- **The block fits.** 4 decoder layers at width 256 is 4.21M parameters in the
  loop body: 1.00 MB at ternary, 2.01 MB at 4-bit, 4.02 MB at int8. Every format
  fits the 4 MB memory tiles, and at ternary the design has three quarters of the
  budget it has not spent.
- **The decode order is now a real decision rather than a preference.** The
  design chose masked diffusion for the NPU because it fills 8 slots per pass
  instead of one (0.7 to 4.5 ms against 4 to 27 ms in the plan's cost model).
  It costs 10 points of goal success at this size. Whether the loop buys them
  back is exactly step 2's question, and level 3 is where to look first.
- **Untouched:** the seed spread (one seed), the crowded suites, retrieval, and
  everything on the NPU. The step-1 gate was the only thing this run was for.

## 5. What changed in the plan

- `PLAN.md` §14 records the gate as passed.
- Step 2's sweep is not run on this model: at 98.6% goal there is no headroom to
  show a loop helping. It runs on a one-layer block, which is both the shape with
  room to move and the shape the NPU wants (`models/tiny/run_step2.sh`).
- Decision 3's format argument is amended by measurement rather than by this run:
  `models/npu/kernels/tern_mk/README.md` found ternary does not dominate 4-bit on
  this silicon, so step 3 sweeps four formats at matched resident bytes rather
  than assuming ternary (`models/tiny/run_step3.sh`).
- The pretrained text encoder stays off the table. The condition for
  reconsidering it was a model that binds on test and fails on the held-out
  world; the held-out world is 2.2 points behind test.
