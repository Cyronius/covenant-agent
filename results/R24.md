# R24: borrowed worlds teach the tiny planner the moves but not the game

**Date:** 2026-09-30. **Branch:** `description-reading`. **Spend:** $0.81
(one secure A40, $0.49/hr, three runs side by side); balance $8.39.
**Plan:** `.claude/plans/borrowed-worlds.md` steps 6-7. Built worlds and the
bar: `results/BORROWED.md`. The owner: "Go".

## The runs

`clt_RD`'s recipe (R23: Ternlight-mini reader, `--pack --seed 0`, 12 epochs)
on three 30,000-row mixes of `clt_train` with new rows at 10, 20 and 30%
(`brw10_RD`, `brw20_RD`, `brw30_RD`). The new rows are the observe-then-act
turns built in steps 1-5: family A re-rendered as one-line briefs, workshop
(crafting), rooms (BabyAI-style grid rooms) and the two trainable service
worlds. Scorer: `results/logs/score_brw.sh`; logs in `results/logs/brw/`.

## The result

1. **The new kinds went from never compiling to mostly well-formed turns.**
   Per-turn exams (play.py, live reader, backoff 3; held-out worlds only):

   | exam | `clt_RD` | `brw10_RD` | `brw20_RD` | `brw30_RD` |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | boatyard (held-out recipes), goal | 1/267 | 25 | 30 | 35 |
   | boatyard, compiles | 209 | 267 | 267 | 267 |
   | rooms_after (held-out wording), goal | 0/202 | 24 | 25 | 38 |
   | rooms_after, compiles | 146 | 202 | 202 | 202 |
   | house (new kind: room graph), goal | 0/445 | 103 | 66 | 40 |
   | house, compiles | 5 | 359 | 404 | 325 |
   | service_telecom, goal | 0/93 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

2. **It reads the options inside a kind it trained on, not in a new one.**
   On turns where the model picked the right tool and the right first argument was
   one of several same-typed options (`I` constants):

   | exam | right option, brw10 / 20 / 30 | chance |
   |---|---|---|
   | boatyard | 68 / 68 / 73% | 6% |
   | house | 48 / 49 / 33% | 42-49% |

   Boatyard is the crafting kind with recipes never seen in training, and the
   model picks the right item among about 16 far above chance. House is a
   kind never trained on, and the model picks at chance. It learned house's
   tools (right first tool on 114-187 of 445 turns, against 3 for `clt_RD`).

   **Correction (same day): the house number does not measure transfer.**
   The house oracle routes to the lamp or key room using the whole map
   (`harness/oracles/house.py:81-103`). The observation shows only the ways
   out of the current room (`runtime/worlds/house.py` `observe`: "the west
   way out of the pantry, to the kitchen, open"). Of the exam's 253 `go`
   labels, 128 head for a lamp or key room the input never mentions and 87
   need the map; only 38 step straight into the goal room. No policy without
   memory could get most of them right, so "at chance" says little about
   whether a kind transfers.

3. **Live play: 0 wins in 108 episodes** (6 worlds x 3 runs x 6, 20 turns
   each; `harness.rpg_suite --planner tiny --exits`). The logs show three
   ways it fails, and none of them is the harness: the live context and the
   training rendering of the same state are byte-identical.
   - **It repeats what just failed.** House: `go` through the locked way,
     20 turns running, with "failed: the down the steps way is locked" in
     the request and "open" written next to the other way. Rooms (a trained
     world): 108 of 114 refused moves repeat the move refused the turn
     before. The per-turn exams cannot see this. Their only post-failure
     turns (14-30 per exam) come from the oracle's detours, and the model
     answers them as well as it answers any other turn.
   - **It skips the steps in between.** Boatyard: gather, then craft the
     boat, "short 1 tar", again, every turn. It never crafts the tar.
     Workshop (trained) is the same, except that `brw30_RD` builds the
     station in 6 of 6 episodes before stalling.
   - **It never attacks.** Dungeon, `brw30_RD`: walks to the key and picks
     it up (6 of 6, against 0 before), then walks back and forth next to a
     goblin, which kills it (6 of 6 dead). Across 222 calls: 216 `move` and 6
     `pick_up`. `brw10_RD` calls `use_item` on a potion it isn't carrying,
     120 turns out of 120. Correction: `attack` exists only in the dungeon
     (`runtime/worlds/rpg.py:88`), which is held out, so no training row
     ever called it. This is a verb never taught, not evidence that rows
     can't teach it.

4. **It costs the old exams, and more with each step up in mix share.**

   | exam | `clt_RD` (R23) | `brw10_RD` | `brw20_RD` | `brw30_RD` |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | plain | 67.0 | 64.4 | 64.6 | 63.3 |
   | decoyed | 47.1 | 43.4 | 43.1 | 43.9 |
   | flip probe | 39.6 (seed 1: 35.2) | 36.4 | 35.2 | 33.9 |
   | cluttered, full half | 61.6 | 58.2 | 55.5 | 51.2 |
   | 500 sample, clean (play.py, backoff) | 73.4 | 70.0 | 69.0 | 70.0 |
   | 500 sample, cluttered (play.py, backoff) | 66.2 | 61.6 | 59.2 | 54.0 |
   | demo requests (of 70) | 10 | 10 | 15 | 15 |

   `test` (each run's own corpus: 85.1 / 78.6 / 70.7) isn't comparable to
   `clt_RD`'s 89.6, because the mixes put their own new rows in it.

   The demo's +5 of 70 is within what 70 tasks can resolve. Plain, decoyed
   and flip lose about 3 points at every mix ratio. The seed-to-seed spread in R23
   was 2-4 points, so the smallest mix is at the edge of noise. Clutter pays
   more at every step up: 5, 11 and 12 points on the cluttered sample.

## What it means

- The rows did what rows can do: the tools, the program shape, and option
  choice by description inside a known kind. They didn't give the model what
  a game needs across turns: change course when the last turn failed, work
  back from a goal to the step before it, and fight when the way is
  blocked. Each turn is a fresh one-line request, and the failure clause is
  a few tokens in it.
- Step 8 (the second batch of worlds) was conditional on the new kinds
  transferring. House can't answer that (see the correction in point 2), so
  transfer to a new kind is still unmeasured. Step 8 waits on the menu in
  `.claude/plans/tiny-general-agent-menu.md`.
- The repeat loop has a plain mechanical cause. A refused move leaves the
  state unchanged, so the next input differs only in its "Last turn:
  failed" clause, and a memoryless greedy policy gives the same answer. The
  corpus barely covers this case: `data/rmb_episodes.jsonl` has 81 failure
  rows, and only 2 follow a refused `move`.
- No mix beats `clt_RD` as the demo planner: each costs 3-12 points on the
  exams it already passes, for a demo gain inside the noise.

## Options, none started

1. ~~Keep `clt_RD` as the demo planner and give the game demos to a bigger
   planner.~~ Rejected by the owner (2026-09-30): the goal is one small
   model that handles many kinds of task, and handing a kind to a bigger
   model does not serve it. The wider search for what would fix this is
   `.claude/plans/tiny-general-agent-menu.md`.
2. **A $0 harness probe: never replay a refused call.** `ar_backoff`
   already retries until a program compiles. Its check could also reject a
   program whose first call is the call refused last turn, so the next
   candidate plays instead. This targets the repetition in point 3 without
   touching the model. It would not fix crafting chains or attacking.
3. **More rows won't fix the cross-turn skills.** Going from 10% to 30% new
   rows raised the per-turn exams by a few points and lowered the old ones by up to 12. A model
   that has to plan a chain needs the chain in its input, or a longer
   horizon than one turn.
